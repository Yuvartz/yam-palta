# Community, partners and press

Sources: Astra's channel table (section 2 of `docs-internal/codex-marketing-plan-2026-09-20.md`, "קהילות, שותפים וגילוי" rows with first-five-posts copy) and `docs-internal/marketing/playbook.md`.

## Etiquette that protects the brand
- Groups (שחייה בים הפתוח, סאפ, שנירקול, טריאתלון): first message goes to the **admins** asking permission; the intro post is in first person ("אני יובל, בניתי…"), useful, with one link. Afterwards at most one helpful post per week, only where the group welcomes it; answer questions, never spam forecasts.
- WhatsApp communities: only through an admin; 1–2 messages/week; forecast messages must carry date, source and the disclaimer.
- Coaches / masters / triathlon: offer a weekly pre-training forecast card; training decisions stay with the coach (say so).
- SUP rentals, beach kiosks, cafés: QR sign pilot with one business first; copy like "קפה יש. פלטה? סורקים ובודקים." Monthly refresh.
- Lifeguard / municipal pages: one formal approach after the product is stable; forecasts complement, lifeguard instructions decide.
- Press (tech / lifestyle): the human story ("the anti-surf app built by a swimmer"), 2 personal pitches per week, one follow-up, never mass mail. Offer a demo link, screenshots from `docs/og-image.png` and a story video.
- Reddit r/Israel and Product Hunt: English, only once the /en/ experience is presentable; answer every comment.

## Draft format
For each contact: audience · goal · channel · the message (Hebrew unless stated) · attachment/link with UTM · what we ask for · what we offer · follow-up date. Save under `docs-internal/marketing/outreach/<date>-<target>.md`. Include a one-line note on why this partner fits the flat-sea audience.

## Never
Send, post, DM or email on the owner's behalf; scrape member lists; promise exclusivity, safety, or rankings; use tracking shorteners; approach minors' groups; misrepresent the app as a safety service.
