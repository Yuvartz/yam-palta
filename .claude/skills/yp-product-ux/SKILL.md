# Product UX for Yam Palata

Work only in this repository, preserving existing product decisions and permissions. No external analytics, services or dependencies.

Identify the task behind each screen: understand conditions at a place and time, compare upcoming periods, or adjust a relevant preference. Present the selected location, applicable time, principal condition and data freshness together. Detailed meteorological information should remain available through clear progressive disclosure.

Map actual journeys before changing layout: returning visitor, switching beach, checking another day, adding a place, changing water comfort preference, recovering from unavailable data. Count unnecessary actions and points of ambiguity rather than chasing minimum clicks at any cost.

Keep the existing distinction between the flatness score and personal water-temperature comfort. Do not suggest that a preference changes the score unless the product really does so. Do not equate calmness with guaranteed swimming safety or convert an index into a probability.

Evaluate whether streaks, installation prompts, feedback requests or notifications compete with the immediate conditions question. Make specific findings from the current UI; their mere existence is not a defect or authority to delete them.

Preserve location, selected day and scroll position across relevant updates. Prefer direct labels and reversible actions. Mark heuristic conclusions as hypotheses until observed with users. Completion requires testing the affected journey, not just presenting a wireframe.
